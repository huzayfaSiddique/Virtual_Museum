About the museum

The Virtual Museum exists to make art feel close — no tickets, no queues, just the works and the stories behind them.

“Art that belongs to everyone should be held in trust for everyone — no tickets, no queues, no walls between you and the work, just slow looking and the stories that carry across centuries.”

— The Curatorial Manifesto of the Virtual Museum

Our story

We began as a student project devoted to public-domain art: painting and sculpture whose copyright has lapsed and which belongs, in the truest sense, to everyone.

The collection is organised into four eras — Renaissance, Impressionism, Modern, and Sculpture — so that visitors can move through art history the way a curator would arrange a gallery wall.
