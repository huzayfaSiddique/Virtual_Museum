Four collections, four centuries — each arranged around a single period and its way of seeing.

Our collections

Explore our four core curatorial wings. Each represents a pivotal era in artistic inquiry, preserved and documented with high-resolution imagery.

1440–1505
Renaissance Wing

Proportion & Devotion

Tempera on poplar and gold ground panels celebrating human anatomy, spiritual devotion, and early linear perspective in Italy and Flanders.

Featured: Fra Angelico, van der Weyden, Memling, Ghirlandaio, Perugino

1871–1906
Impressionism Wing

Light & Atmosphere

Dissolving form into radiant colour. French open-air landscape studies, rainy Parisian boulevards, and pointillist optics by the water.

Featured: Monet, Seurat, Caillebotte, Pissarro, van Gogh

Explore Impressionism (5 works)
1893–1925
Modern Wing

Form & Fracture

Breaking the traditional picture plane through Fauvist color fields, expressive Northern European portraiture, and pure neoplastic geometry.

Featured: Matisse, Kandinsky, Mondrian, Munch, Modigliani

Explore Modern (5 works)
1778–1920
Sculpture Wing

Volume & Materiality

Returning art to three physical dimensions: neoclassical Carrara marble, dramatic bronze combat studies, and monumental civic commemoration.

Featured: Canova, Barye, Houdon, French, Saint-Gaudens
